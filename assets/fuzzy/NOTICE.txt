char_readings.txt lists how Han characters are read in Taiwanese and in Mandarin, without tones. It is made by tool/build_char_readings.py.

Lines starting with T (Taiwanese) are adapted from ../taigi/reminder_readings.json and distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); see ../taigi/NOTICE.txt for its sources.
Changes: gave each character of a word the syllable it is read as in that word, and removed the tones.

Lines starting with M (Mandarin) are adapted from pypinyin (https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin), which takes its readings from pinyin-data (https://github.com/mozillazg/pinyin-data) and phrase-pinyin-data (https://github.com/mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data).
Changes: removed the tones and kept, for each character, its first reading and the other readings in common use.
